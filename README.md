# Machine Learning Engineer Interview Guide (Graduate Level)

A single offline HTML (HyperText Markup Language) file with **164 interview questions**, written explanations for every answer, and **16 mixed practice papers**. Built for graduate and entry-level Machine Learning Engineer (MLE) interviews in the United Kingdom.

## Please read this first

I am an MSc (Master of Science) Computer Science student, not an authority on this subject. I built this guide while preparing for these interviews myself, and I am sharing it because I could not find one resource that covered everything with proper explanations.

**Some of it may be wrong.** The explanations reflect my current understanding plus research. I have reviewed the content, but I am still learning, and in places a specialist would want more nuance than I have given. Use it to revise and to find your gaps, not as something to quote in an interview.

## How it works

- **Every answer is explained.** Submit a question and you get a verdict, why the correct answer is correct, and a rebuttal aimed specifically at the option you picked. The explanation appears whether you were right or wrong.
- **Practice papers mix the topics.** Each of the 16 papers takes ten questions from ten different sections, the way a real interview jumps around. No question appears in two papers.
- **Answer positions are shuffled** so you cannot pass by always guessing (b).
- **Scores per section and an overall progress bar.** Use "Reset section" or "Reset paper" to sit it again cold a few days later.
- **Light and dark themes**, following your system setting on first visit.

## How to use it

1. Open the link above, or download `index.html` and open it in any browser. It works with no internet connection.
2. Work through one section per sitting: read the notes, answer the questions without looking back, then read every explanation in full.
3. Once you have covered the sections, sit the practice papers cold. Aim for eight out of ten before an interview.
4. Below six on a paper, go back to the sections those questions came from. Each question is tagged with its source.

## Technical notes

One file, no dependencies, no build step, no network requests. Plain HTML, CSS (Cascading Style Sheets) and JavaScript. Only your theme choice is stored in the browser, and nothing is sent anywhere. All colour pairs in both themes meet WCAG (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines) AA contrast for body text.

To run it locally, clone the repository and open `index.html`. That is the whole process.
