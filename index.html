    Machine Learning Engineer interview guide

    A complete, self-contained revision resource for graduate Machine Learning Engineer (MLE) interviews. Read each section, then answer the closed questions. Every answer, right or wrong, comes with a written explanation of why the correct option works and why your chosen option does not.

    Works offline

    One file, no internet connection needed. Open it on a laptop or a phone.

    Explains every answer

    Feedback appears whether you are right or wrong, so wrong answers still teach you something.

    Mixed practice papers

    Ten-question papers that jump between topics, the way a real interview does.

    Please read: accuracy and how this was made. I built this while preparing for graduate Machine Learning Engineer (MLE) interviews myself. I am a student, not an authority, and the explanations reflect my current understanding plus research, drafted with the help of an Artificial Intelligence (AI) assistant and then reviewed by me. Some of it may be wrong, out of date, or simplified in a way a specialist would object to. Treat it as revision material to think with, not as a reference to quote in an interview. If you spot an error, please open an issue on the repository and I will fix it and credit you.
    How to use this guide. Work through one section per sitting. Read the notes first, attempt the questions without looking back, then read the explanations in full. Use the revision summary at the end of each section the night before an interview. Use "Reset section" to clear your answers and try again a few days later.

    Built as a single offline HTML (HyperText Markup Language) file. Your theme choice and nothing else is stored in your browser. No analytics, no network requests, no data leaves your device.
